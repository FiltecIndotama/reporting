The short answer

⚠ ERPNext cannot answer this question today — sales-staff attribution is not recorded on Sales Orders.

Every field ERPNext provides for crediting a salesperson on a Sales Order is empty or generic. Of 230 orders, 3 carry any Sales Person at all — and those name the group Sales Team, not a person. So no report built on the standard ERPNext fields can attribute orders to individuals.

There are also only 3 Sales Person records in the system — Sales Team (group), TIM FITT and Tim FIT. These are teams, not people, so even a correctly filled sales_team table could not resolve to an individual today.

How to make this report real

Four changes in ERPNext turn this from an inference into a fact. None require custom code.

  1. Create one Sales Person record per rep. Today there are three, and they are teams (Sales Team, TIM FITT, Tim FIT). Add each rep, linked to their Employee record, under the right team as parent.
  2. Make the Sales Team table mandatory on Quotation and Sales Order. Set sales_team as required (or add a validation hook) so an order cannot be submitted without a named Sales Person and an allocated percentage.
  3. Stop entering orders from one shared account. 225 of 230 orders were created by haerul@filtec.co.id and the sales@filtec.co.id mailbox is shared. Give each rep their own login so owner becomes a usable fallback signal.
  4. Always create Sales Orders from their Quotation. That carries the sales team through automatically and preserves the trail this report had to reconstruct by hand.

Once (1) and (2) are in place, the native ERPNext report Sales Person-wise Transaction Summary answers this question directly, with per-line contribution percentages — no custom reporting needed.
